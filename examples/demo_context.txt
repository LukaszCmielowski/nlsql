This is a fictional electronics shop. Use only the nlsql_demo schema.

Tables and relationships:
- nlsql_demo.customers: one row per customer. Primary key: customer_id.
  customer_name is the person's display name; country contains full country names.
- nlsql_demo.orders: one row per order. Primary key: order_id.
  orders.customer_id references customers.customer_id.
  ordered_on is the order date. status is completed, pending, or cancelled.
- nlsql_demo.order_items: one row per order line. Primary key: (order_id, line_number).
  order_items.order_id references orders.order_id.
  Each line has a product_name, category, quantity, and unit_price.

Business rules:
- All monetary values are USD. Revenue is SUM(quantity * unit_price) for completed orders only.
- Pending and cancelled orders do not contribute to revenue or units sold.
- Customer spending means revenue from that customer's completed orders.
- Count orders from the order headers; after joining lines, use COUNT(DISTINCT order_id).
- Include customers with no orders when asked about all customers or customers who never ordered.
- No tax, discounts, shipping charges, or exchange rates are modeled.
- The fixed demo dates cover January through April 2026; do not assume the data is current.

Examples of useful questions:
- What is total completed revenue?
- Which three customers spent the most?
- What was completed revenue by country?
- Show completed revenue by month for January through April 2026.
- Which products sold the most units in completed orders?
- Which customers have never placed an order?
- How many orders are pending or cancelled?
